Bio

Vânia Moreira is Associate Professor of Pharmaceutical Chemistry at the Faculty of Pharmacy, University of Coimbra, Portugal, and a Visiting Researcher at the Faculty of Pharmacy, University of Helsinki, Finland. She holds a PhD in Pharmacy from the University of Coimbra (2008), undertaken in collaboration with the University of Maryland School of Medicine, Baltimore, USA, and completed with the highest-scoring grade, "Passed with Distinction and Honours."

She is a Fellow of the Higher Education Academy (FHEA, UK) and holds the Finnish academic title of Docent (Dosentti) in Pharmaceutical Chemistry from the University of Helsinki. Her career spans research and teaching positions across Portugal, Finland and the United Kingdom, including as University Lecturer B (Chancellor's Research Fellow) at the University of Strathclyde and as Principal Investigator at the University of Helsinki. She currently serves as Vice-President of the GP2A (Group for the Promotion of Pharmaceutical Chemistry in Academia).
